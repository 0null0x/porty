Porty

A real 64-bit Android (Android-x86 9.0) running on your device, inside this browser tab. No app, no server: a PC is emulated by QEMU compiled to WebAssembly.

Checking this browser...

  • Experimental and slow. Every x86 instruction is translated in the browser. Booting takes minutes, not seconds, and may not finish on a phone.
  • Large download. Tens of MB to start, then several hundred MB of the system image are fetched on demand while Android boots. Use Wi-Fi.
  • Needs memory. 1 GB or more of RAM is reserved for the guest. Close other tabs first.
  • Live session. Nothing is saved. Reloading the page starts from scratch.
  • No Google Play, no Google apps. This is the open-source Android-x86 image.
  • No network, no cellular, no SMS, no calls, no camera, no sound.
